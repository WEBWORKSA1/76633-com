Support FAQ

Where does my contribution go?

Hosting and tools, new calculators, video production, translations (Hindi, Spanish, Chinese next), contest prizes and paying freelance writers and editors.

Is my support tax-deductible?

76633.com is an independent publication, not a registered charity, so contributions are not tax-deductible.

Can I support without paying online?

Yes — send a pledge with the form and we will reply with bank, UPI or invoice options. Sharing the site also helps enormously.
